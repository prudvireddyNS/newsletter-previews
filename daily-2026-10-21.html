Google and Meta’s TorchTPU is a direct attempt to make TPUs less foreign to PyTorch teams: eager execution, torch.compile integration, and simple .to("tpu") migration. The practical win is optionality against Nvidia GPUs; peak performance still needs TPU-aware shapes, sharding, and ops.

DeepSeek has open sourced DeepInfer, the inference framework it says powers DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at scale. vLLM notes it was initially built primarily on vLLM and released under Apache 2.0, making this more than a paper release for inference-stack teams.

NVIDIA pushes generative simulation into clinical surgical scenes

NVIDIA’s Cosmos3-H-Dreams moves generative simulation from tabletop surgical robotics into clinical cholecystectomy scenes, using video plus robot kinematics and streaming on one RTX PRO 6000. Useful research substrate, but NVIDIA is explicit: visual realism is not biomechanical validation and this is not for patient care.

Anthropic’s draft AI safety bill would define catastrophic model-release risk around 50+ deaths or $1B in damage, but TechCrunch’s read is that federal agencies could not block release without a judge. It is regulation language from a lab, so incentives matter as much as thresholds.

Ai2’s FairStress argues fairness evaluations should test how easily models abandon decisions under pressure, not just final choices. The benchmark exposes demographic asymmetries in generated decisions, explanations, and some activation analyses.
